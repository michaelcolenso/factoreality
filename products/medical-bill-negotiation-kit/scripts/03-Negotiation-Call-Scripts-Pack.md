# Negotiation Call Scripts Pack

*Companion to Modules 2, 3 & 5. Word-for-word scripts for the calls that lower your bill. Keep your case file and a pen ready — fill in the call log every time.*

> **Disclaimer:** Educational scripts only — not legal advice. Outcomes vary. Stay calm, factual, and specific; specificity is leverage.

---

## Universal call-log template (fill in for EVERY call)

```
Date: ________   Time: ________
Who I called: ____________________  (Phone: __________)
Rep name / ID: ___________________
Reason for call: _________________________________________
What they said: __________________________________________
What they agreed to: _____________________________________
Reference / ticket #: ____________________________________
Next step / callback date: _______________________________
```

> Golden rule: **get a name and a reference number on every call**, and follow up any promise with a short confirming email.

---

## 1. Provider / billing office — itemized bill + first errors

> "Hi, my name is [name], account [#]. I'm reviewing my bill and I have a couple of items to correct. On the itemized statement, line [#], code [CPT], dated [date], appears to be **billed twice** — can you remove the duplicate? Also, [code/description] doesn't appear in my medical record — can you provide documentation for it or remove it? Can you send me a **corrected itemized statement** once those are fixed, and give me a reference number?"

**If they push back:** "I understand. Can I speak with a billing supervisor, or be connected to your patient advocate / billing compliance office? I want to get this corrected."

---

## 2. Self-pay / prompt-pay / uninsured discount

> "I'm handling this bill myself. Do you offer a **self-pay or uninsured discount**, or a **prompt-pay discount** if I take care of this quickly? What discount can you apply to account [#]?"

**Anchor with data:** "I've looked at what insurers and Medicare reimburse for these codes, and the billed amount is well above that. I'm asking you to bring this in line with a fair rate."

---

## 3. Lump-sum settlement offer

> "I want to resolve this account. I can pay **$[X] as a lump sum today** to settle the balance in full. Can you accept that as payment in full?"

**Negotiating the number:**
- Open low (e.g., ~40–50% of the clean balance) to leave room.
- If they counter, move up slowly toward your target.
- Before paying anything: "Can you send me written confirmation that **$[X] settles this account in full** and that there will be **no negative credit reporting**? I'll pay as soon as I have that in writing."

**Lock the terms (say this back to them):** "So to confirm: I pay $[X], the account is **paid in full / fully satisfied**, nothing further is owed, and there's **no negative reporting**. Please email that to [email]."

---

## 4. Hardship-based reduction (bridge to financial assistance)

> "My financial situation makes this balance very difficult. Can you **reduce the balance** based on hardship, and can you tell me about your **financial-assistance / charity-care program**? Please send me the Financial Assistance Policy and application, and place a **hold on collections** while I apply."

---

## 5. Insurance — understand & appeal a denial (Module 5)

> "I'm calling about claim [#], date of service [date]. The EOB shows it was **denied** for reason [code/description]. Can you explain that in plain language? **Exactly what do you need to overturn it**, and what's the **deadline to appeal**? Is there an internal appeal and an external review? Please send me that in writing."

**For 'not medically necessary':** "I'll be submitting a letter of medical necessity from my doctor with the appeal. What's the address/portal and the filing deadline?"

---

## 6. Payment plan (Module 6)

> "After the adjustments we discussed, the balance is $[X]. I'd like to set up an **interest-free payment plan** I can sustain — I can pay **$[Y] per month**. Can we do that with **0% interest and no fees**, keep the account **out of collections and off my credit** while I pay as agreed, and can you **email me the plan in writing**?"

---

## Handling common pushback (quick answers)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "That's just our price." | "That's the list price; insurers pay far less. What's the best self-pay rate you can do?" |
| "We can't go any lower." | "I appreciate that. Can you connect me to a supervisor or financial assistance?" |
| "You must pay in full for the discount." | "If I can fund a lump sum today, what amount settles this in full?" |
| "Policy doesn't allow it." | "Who can make an exception — a supervisor or patient advocate?" |
| "We'll note that on your account." | "Thank you — please email me written confirmation and a reference number." |

---

## After every successful call

1. Get it **in writing** (corrected statement, settlement letter, or plan agreement).
2. **Don't pay** a settlement until the written terms arrive.
3. File the confirmation in your case file.
4. Calendar the next follow-up.
