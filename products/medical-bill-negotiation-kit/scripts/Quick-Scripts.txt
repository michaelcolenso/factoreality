============================================================
 THE MEDICAL BILL NEGOTIATION & DISPUTE KIT
 QUICK SCRIPTS (plain text) — copy/paste or read aloud
============================================================
Educational self-advocacy only. Not legal/medical/financial
advice. Outcomes vary. Verify rules for your situation.
Get a name + reference number on EVERY call. Get every deal
in writing BEFORE you pay.
------------------------------------------------------------

[1] REQUEST AN ITEMIZED BILL + PAUSE THE CLOCK
"Hi, my name is ____, account ____. I'd like a FULLY ITEMIZED
statement with all billing codes, quantities, and unit prices,
plus a copy of my medical records for [date]. While I review it,
please place a HOLD on the due date and any collections. Can I
get a reference number and your name?"

[2] DISPUTE AN ERROR
"On the itemized bill, line ___, code ___, dated ___, appears to
be [billed twice / not in my medical record / wrong quantity].
Please [remove it / correct it / provide documentation] and send
me a CORRECTED itemized statement. Reference number, please?"

[3] WRONG / MISSING INSURANCE
"This was processed as self-pay, but I was insured on the date of
service. Please rebill [insurer], member ID ____. Also confirm any
secondary insurance was billed."

[4] BILLED ABOVE THE ALLOWED RATE (IN-NETWORK)
"My EOB shows the allowed amount is $____. As an in-network
provider you can't balance-bill me above that. Please adjust."

[5] ASK FOR A DISCOUNT
"Do you offer a self-pay, uninsured, or prompt-pay discount? What
discount can you apply to account ____?"

[6] LUMP-SUM SETTLEMENT
"I can pay $____ as a lump sum today to settle this in full. Can
you accept that? Please EMAIL me written confirmation that $____
settles the account IN FULL with NO negative credit reporting,
and I'll pay as soon as I receive it."

[7] HARDSHIP + FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
"My situation makes this very hard to pay. Can you reduce the
balance for hardship, and please send me your FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
POLICY and application. Please HOLD collections while I apply."

[8] INTEREST-FREE PAYMENT PLAN
"After our adjustments the balance is $____. I can pay $____ per
month at 0% interest with no fees. Can we set that up, keep the
account out of collections and off my credit while I pay as
agreed, and EMAIL me the agreement in writing?"

[9] INSURANCE DENIAL — UNDERSTAND & APPEAL
"Claim ____, date ____, was DENIED for reason ____. Please explain
it plainly. EXACTLY what's needed to overturn it, and what's the
DEADLINE to appeal? Is there an internal appeal and an external
review? Please send that in writing."

[10] SURPRISE / BALANCE BILL
"This appears to be a surprise/balance bill protected under the
federal No Surprises Act. Please reprocess so I owe only my
in-network cost-sharing, and send a corrected statement."

[11] DEBT COLLECTOR — FIRST CONTACT
"I'm not confirming this debt. Please send me written validation:
the amount, the original creditor, an itemized statement, and
proof you're authorized to collect. Until then, please cease
collection and do not report this to the credit bureaus.
Send all communication in writing to [address]."
