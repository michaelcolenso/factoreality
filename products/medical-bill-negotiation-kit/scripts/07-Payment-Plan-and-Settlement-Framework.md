# Payment Plan Negotiation Script + Settlement Framework

*Companion to Modules 3 & 6. Use after errors are removed (Module 2), discounts/settlement explored (Module 3), and financial assistance applied for (Module 4). Negotiate the **reduced** balance.*

> **Disclaimer:** Educational tool only — not legal or financial advice. Never pay a settlement until you have the agreed terms in writing.

---

## Part A — Lump-Sum Settlement Framework

### The four numbers

1. **Clean balance** — after errors removed and insurance correctly applied: $______
2. **Target** — a fair amount anchored to insurer-allowed/Medicare rates: $______
3. **Opening offer** — below target (often ~40–50% of clean balance): $______
4. **Walk-away** — the most you can actually fund as a lump sum: $______

### Script

> "I'd like to settle account [#] today. I can pay **$[opening]** as a lump sum to close this in full. Can you accept that?"

- If countered, move up slowly toward your target.
- Never exceed your walk-away.

### Lock the deal (before paying — read it back)

> "To confirm: I pay **$[agreed]**, the account is **paid in full and fully satisfied**, nothing more is owed, and there is **no negative credit reporting**. Please **email that confirmation to [email]** and I'll pay as soon as I receive it."

### Settlement confirmation must state

- [ ] Agreed settlement amount
- [ ] That it **resolves the balance in full** ("paid in full" / "in full satisfaction")
- [ ] **No further amounts owed**
- [ ] **No negative / derogatory credit reporting**
- [ ] Account number and date
- [ ] Then: pay by a traceable method and **keep the receipt + the letter forever**

## Part B — Interest-Free Payment Plan

### Script

> "After our adjustments, the balance is **$[X]**. I want to pay this responsibly. I can commit to **$[Y] per month** with **0% interest and no fees**. Can we set that up, keep the account **out of collections and off my credit** while I pay as agreed, and **email me the agreement in writing**?"

### Hold your number

> "I understand you'd prefer more per month, but a payment I can sustain is one I won't miss. $[Y]/month is what I can reliably pay."

### Written plan agreement checklist

- [ ] Total balance after all adjustments
- [ ] Monthly payment amount + due date
- [ ] **0% interest, no fees** (explicit)
- [ ] Number of payments / payoff date
- [ ] **No prepayment penalty**
- [ ] Account stays **out of collections and off credit reporting** while current
- [ ] What happens if a payment is late, and how to cure it
- [ ] How payments are applied + how you get receipts
- [ ] Account contact name/number

---

## Part C — Avoid the traps

- ❌ **Don't reflexively use a medical credit card** with deferred interest — a missed payoff can trigger large retroactive interest. Ask for the provider's **in-house 0% plan** first.
- ❌ **Don't agree to an unaffordable monthly amount** to "get it over with"; defaulting can send it to collections.
- ❌ **Don't pay a settlement before the written terms arrive.**
- ✅ **Do** combine: errors removed → discount/settlement → financial assistance → then a plan on whatever remains.
