# Financial Hardship / Charity Care Application Guide + Letter Template

*Companion to Module 4. Many (especially nonprofit) hospitals must offer financial assistance — sometimes full forgiveness. Most patients never apply. You will.*

> **Disclaimer:** Educational guide only — not legal or financial advice. Eligibility rules, thresholds, and deadlines vary by hospital and state and change over time. Verify the policy that applies to you.

---

## Step 1 — Get the policy and the deadline

- [ ] Request the hospital's **Financial Assistance Policy (FAP)**, its **plain-language summary**, and the **application form** (call, or search "[hospital name] financial assistance policy")
- [ ] Note the **application deadline** (often months after the first post-discharge bill; sometimes you can apply even after a bill is in collections)
- [ ] Ask for a **hold on billing/collections while your application is under review**

## Step 2 — Check whether you might qualify (don't self-disqualify)

Eligibility usually depends on **household income vs. the Federal Poverty Level (FPL)**, sometimes plus assets and the size of the bill relative to income. Many hospitals offer:

- **Full charity care** below one income threshold
- **Sliding-scale discounts** above it
- **Catastrophic relief** when bills exceed a share of your annual income — even at higher incomes

> Apply even if you think you earn "too much." Thresholds are often higher than people expect, and catastrophic provisions exist for exactly this.

## Step 3 — Gather your documentation

- [ ] Photo ID + proof of address
- [ ] Recent **pay stubs** (often last 1–3 months) and/or employer letter
- [ ] Most recent **tax return** + W-2s/1099s
- [ ] Recent **bank statements**
- [ ] Proof of other income (Social Security, unemployment, disability, pension)
- [ ] Proof of benefits / hardship (Medicaid denial, SNAP, etc.) — can speed approval
- [ ] The completed **application form**
- [ ] A short **cover letter** (template below)

## Step 4 — Submit with proof, and follow up relentlessly

- [ ] Submit via certified mail or portal upload — **keep a copy and proof of submission**
- [ ] **Confirm receipt** within a few days; ask if anything's missing
- [ ] **Follow up every 1–2 weeks**; get the reviewer's name and an expected decision date
- [ ] If **denied**, ask **why in writing**, whether you can appeal, and whether a different (e.g., catastrophic) provision applies — denials are often a missing document

## Step 5 — Don't overlook outside help

- [ ] Nonprofit patient-advocacy organizations (often free help with hospital aid)
- [ ] Disease-specific foundations / medication grant programs
- [ ] **Medicaid, including retroactive Medicaid** for past bills if you were eligible at the time

## Cover Letter Template

```
[Your Name]
[Address] | [Phone] | [Email]
[Date]

[Hospital Financial Assistance / Patient Financial Services]
[Address]

RE: Application for Financial Assistance / Charity Care
Patient: [Name]   Account #: [#]   Date(s) of Service: [Dates]

To the Financial Assistance Department:

I am requesting financial assistance under your Financial Assistance
Policy for the account referenced above. [One or two plain sentences about
your situation — e.g., "I am currently [unemployed / on a fixed income /
supporting a family of __ on $____ per year] and am unable to pay this
balance in full."]

Enclosed please find my completed application and supporting documentation:
[list what you're including — ID, pay stubs, tax return, bank statements,
proof of benefits].

Please confirm receipt of this application and let me know if anything
further is needed. I also request that billing and collection activity on
this account be paused while my application is reviewed.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Printed Name]
```

## Hardship Letter (short statement of need — attach if no form field exists)

```
To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to explain my financial hardship regarding account [#].
My household consists of [#] people. My monthly income is approximately
$[___], and my essential monthly expenses (housing, utilities, food,
transportation, other medical costs) total approximately $[___], leaving
little or nothing for medical debt. [Add any relevant circumstances:
job loss, illness, reduced hours, dependents, other debts.]

I am asking that you reduce or forgive this balance under your financial
assistance policy, or place me on the most affordable terms available.
I am committed to resolving this account in good faith within my means.

Sincerely,
[Name] | [Date]
```
