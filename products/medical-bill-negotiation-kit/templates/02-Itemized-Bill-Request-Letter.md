# Itemized Bill Request Letter Template

*Companion to Module 1. Send by email or certified mail. A plain-text version (`.txt`) is included in the `scripts/` folder for quick copy-paste. Replace every [BRACKETED] field.*

> **Disclaimer:** Educational template only — not legal advice. Keep a copy of everything you send; never send original documents.

---

```
[Your Full Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Phone] | [Email]
[Date]

[Provider / Hospital Billing Department]
[Billing Department Address]

RE: Request for Itemized Bill and Records
Patient: [Patient Name]
Date(s) of Service: [Dates]
Account / Bill Number: [Account #]
Date of Birth: [DOB]

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing to request a fully itemized statement for the account
referenced above. The summary bill I received does not provide enough
detail for me to review the charges or verify them against my insurance
Explanation of Benefits.

Please send me the following:

1. A fully itemized bill listing every individual charge, including for
   each line: the date of service, the procedure/service description, the
   billing codes (CPT/HCPCS, revenue codes, and NDC drug codes), the
   quantity, and the unit price.

2. A copy of my medical records for the date(s) of service above, so I can
   confirm that the billed services match the care I received. (I
   understand a reasonable, cost-based fee may apply for records.)

3. Confirmation of which insurance was billed, including any secondary
   coverage, and the amounts billed to and paid by each.

While I review this itemized statement, I respectfully request that you
place a hold on the payment due date and on any collection activity for
this account until I have received the itemized bill and had a reasonable
opportunity to review it. I intend to resolve this account in good faith.

Please send the itemized statement to the address or email above. If you
have questions, you can reach me at [phone] or [email].

Thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
```

---

## Quick-call version

> "Hi, I received a summary bill for account [#] and I'd like to request a **fully itemized statement** with all the billing codes, quantities, and unit prices, plus a copy of my medical records for [date]. While I review it, can you please place a **hold on the due date and any collections**? Can I get a reference number for this request, and your name?"

**Always record in your call log:** date, time, rep's name/ID, reference number, and any hold confirmed.
