# Insurance Denial Appeal Letter Pack

*Companion to Module 5. Includes a Level 1 internal-appeal letter, an escalation/Level 2 variant, and a doctor's letter-of-medical-necessity request. Replace every [BRACKETED] field.*

> **Disclaimer:** Educational templates only — not legal or medical advice. Appeal rights, processes, and deadlines differ by plan type (employer, marketplace, Medicare, Medicaid). Use the deadlines and addresses in **your** denial letter and plan documents, and file within the deadline.

---

## Before you write

1. Find the **denial reason / code** on your EOB or denial letter.
2. Confirm the **filing deadline** and whether an **expedited (urgent)** appeal applies.
3. Decide the denial bucket: clerical (often fixed by provider rebilling), no prior auth, "not medically necessary," "experimental/not covered," or out-of-network/surprise bill.
4. Gather: plan document pages showing coverage, chart notes, test results, prior-auth references, and (for medical-necessity denials) a **letter of medical necessity** from your doctor.

## Letter A — Level 1 Internal Appeal

```
[Your Name]
[Address] | [Phone] | [Email]
[Date]

[Insurance Company — Appeals Department]
[Address from your denial letter]

RE: Internal Appeal of Claim Denial
Member: [Name]      Member ID: [#]
Group #: [#]        Claim #: [#]
Date(s) of Service: [Dates]   Provider: [Provider]
Denial reason/code: [as stated on EOB/denial letter]

To the Appeals Department:

I am formally appealing the denial of the claim referenced above. I
believe this claim should be covered for the following reasons:

[State your case clearly and specifically. Examples:
 - "This service was medically necessary. My physician's letter of
   medical necessity is enclosed, documenting [diagnosis] and why this
   care was required."
 - "My plan covers this service under [section/page of plan document],
   enclosed and highlighted."
 - "This was emergency care and is covered under my plan's emergency
   provisions."
 - "Prior authorization was obtained; the reference number is [#]." ]

Enclosed in support of this appeal:
- [Letter of medical necessity from Dr. ___]
- [Relevant medical records / test results]
- [Plan document pages showing coverage]
- [Any prior-authorization documentation]

Please reverse this denial and process the claim for payment. Per my
appeal rights, please respond in writing within the required timeframe and
advise me of the outcome and any further appeal rights, including external
review.

If any information is missing, please contact me before deciding so I can
provide it. Thank you.

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Printed Name]
```

## Letter B — Escalation / Level 2 (or request for External Review)

```
[Your Name] — [Address] — [Phone] — [Email]
[Date]

[Insurance Company — Grievance/Appeals] OR [State External Review / IRO]

RE: [Second-Level Appeal] / [Request for Independent External Review]
Member: [Name]   Member ID: [#]   Claim #: [#]
Date(s) of Service: [Dates]
Prior appeal reference: [# / date of Level 1 decision]

To Whom It May Concern:

My first-level appeal of the above claim was denied on [date]. I am
[escalating to a second-level appeal] / [requesting an independent
external review], as is my right.

The denial remains incorrect because: [restate the strongest points
concisely and address the specific reason the insurer gave for upholding
the denial]. The enclosed documentation supports coverage:
[list enclosures].

I request a full, independent review of this claim and a written decision
within the required timeframe. Please confirm receipt of this request.

Sincerely,
[Signature] [Printed Name]
```

> **External review note:** If internal appeals fail, you generally have the right to an **independent external review**, whose decision is typically binding on the insurer. Your denial letter and state insurance department explain how to request it. Your **state Department of Insurance / Consumer Assistance Program** can also help; for self-funded employer plans, federal labor rules apply.

## Letter C — Request to Your Doctor for a Letter of Medical Necessity

```
Dear Dr. [Name],

My insurer ([insurer]) denied coverage for [service/treatment] on [date],
citing "[denial reason]." I am appealing and would be grateful for a
letter of medical necessity to include with my appeal.

If possible, please address:
- My diagnosis and relevant history
- Why this specific service was medically necessary for me
- Why alternatives were inadequate or inappropriate
- Any clinical guidelines or evidence supporting this care
- The expected consequences of not receiving it

The insurer's appeal deadline is [date], so I would appreciate the letter
by [date if possible]. Thank you very much for your help.

[Your Name] — [DOB] — [Member ID] — [Phone]
```
