# The Medical Bill Negotiation & Dispute Kit — Package Contents

**Product:** The Medical Bill Negotiation & Dispute Kit
**Type:** resource-guide (self-advocacy system)
**A practical, step-by-step system for U.S. patients to audit, dispute, negotiate, and reduce medical bills — and defend their credit.**

> **Disclaimer:** This kit is an educational self-advocacy resource. It is **not legal, medical, tax, or financial advice**, and no outcome is guaranteed. Rules and deadlines vary by state, plan, and provider and change over time — verify what applies to your situation, and consult a licensed professional for advice on your specific case.

---

## Start here

1. Open **`The-Medical-Bill-Negotiation-and-Dispute-Kit.md`** (the core guide).
2. Read **"If you only have 20 minutes"** and **"How to use this kit."**
3. Work the **7 modules in order**, using the templates and scripts as you go.

If you're in a hurry, keep **`scripts/Quick-Scripts.txt`** open beside you — it has copy-paste phone scripts for every situation.

## Format notes

These deliverables are authored in **Markdown** (`.md`) and **plain text** (`.txt`) for maximum portability and easy editing. To produce the spec's PDF/DOCX formats, convert with a tool such as Pandoc, e.g.:

```bash
pandoc The-Medical-Bill-Negotiation-and-Dispute-Kit.md -o kit.pdf      # needs a LaTeX engine
pandoc templates/02-Itemized-Bill-Request-Letter.md -o request-letter.docx
```
